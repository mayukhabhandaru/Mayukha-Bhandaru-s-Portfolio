Blog #2 Progress

ctrl+alt+delete

This week brought lots of setbacks as well as progress through some hands-on technical work, research, and collaborative peer feedback. After encountering persistent technical difficulties with my initial site build (wix, oh the horror!) I made the executive decision to restart from scratch and code the entire website manually. Through VS Code, Claude, and Github, starting over allowed me to build a far more stable, custom layout, and I successfully developed the full structure and design through pure code.

As well as web development, I pushed forward on my academic goals by finishing my research required for the ISM research assessment, carefully using sources to provide the best summary of the topics. It was quite interesting because of the previous research that I did on industry trends, so seeing it all connect made me realize the whole point of this program in the first place.

Finally, I focused on professional development through a resume exchange—I reviewed and provided constructive feedback on several peers' resumes, gaining fresh perspectives on resume formatting and word choice. In return, I received valuable critiques on my own resume, which I immediately used to refine and polish my experience section. Next week, I plan to continue building out the secondary pages of my website and begin researching more professionals to contact.
