# Quick Start Guide

## Immediate Next Steps

### 1. View Your Website
Double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser and see your new ISM portfolio!

### 2. Essential Customizations (5 minutes)

Open `index.html` in a text editor and find/replace these:

| Find | Replace With |
|------|-------------|
| `[Your Name]` | Your actual name |
| `[Your Research Topic]` | Your ISM research topic |
| `your.email@example.com` | Your email address |

### 3. Choose Your Colors (Optional)

Open `css/styles.css` and modify line 13-15:
```css
--primary-color: #2563eb;    /* Change to your brand color */
--accent-color: #f59e0b;     /* Change to your accent color */
```

### 4. Add Your First Blog Post

In `index.html`, find the first blog entry (around line 320) and update:
- Week number and date
- Blog title
- Blog content

### 5. Deploy Your Site

**Easiest Option - GitHub Pages:**
1. Create a GitHub account (if you don't have one)
2. Create a new repository
3. Upload all files from this folder
4. Go to Settings → Pages → Enable GitHub Pages
5. Share your live URL!

## That's It!

Your ISM portfolio is ready to showcase your journey. Update it weekly as you progress through the program.

Need more details? Check `README.md` for comprehensive documentation.
