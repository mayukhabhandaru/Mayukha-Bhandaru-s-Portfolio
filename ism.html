About ISM

The Independent Study Mentorship (ISM) is an advanced academic program that allows motivated students to explore their professional interests through hands-on experience and mentorship.

What is ISM?

ISM provides students the opportunity to pursue independent research in a field of their choice while working alongside industry professionals. The program combines classroom learning with real-world application.

Mentorship Component

Students are paired with experienced professionals who guide them through their research journey, providing insights, expertise, and valuable industry connections.

Research & Projects

Participants conduct original research, complete assessments including interviews and observations, and develop a final product showcasing their work.

Program Goals

Develop professional skills, gain industry experience, conduct meaningful research, and prepare for future career opportunities in chosen field.
