Blog #3 Progress

The 3 P’s: Polishing, Publishing, & Practicing

This week in ISM wasn’t as hectic as the last, but one accomplishment did stand out: I completed another research project relating to my field— specifically on AI, ethics, and the ethical use of AI in the field of neuroscience. My first research assessment that asked us to research current industry trends in the field got me thinking: when AI is being so widely used in healthcare, is there no concern of HIPAA laws and violating patient privacy? Where do healthcare workers decide to draw the line? This topic pushed me to think beyond AI itself. AI has the innate potential to advance brain research, from being able to analyze complex data to supporting new discoveries. However, it raises serious questions about patient privacy: to what extent is it applicable to use AI and when does it become a violation of HIPAA laws? Neuroscience deals with the most personal information relating to healthcare as a whole: our thoughts and brain activity, so the stakes are especially high. Digging deeper into these issues showed that innovation and ethics must work together in order to move forward and create progress in this field. Besides this, I finalized my resume using the peer feedback I received, fully got my website up to date, and practiced mock interviews with classmates to prepare for next week’s symposium.

Overall, the research project was definitely my favorite part of these 2 weeks, but I'm also looking forward to our ISM symposium next week—I can’t wait to actually put all this prep into action, get feedback, and (potentially) a mentor!!
